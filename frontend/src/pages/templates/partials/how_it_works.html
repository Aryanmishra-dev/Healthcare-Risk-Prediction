Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a medical diagnosis?

No. These are probabilistic estimates based on population-level clinical data. Always consult a qualified doctor for actual diagnosis and treatment.

Is my data stored?

No. Uploaded files are parsed for feature extraction only and are never saved. Form inputs are used only to generate your prediction.

How accurate are the predictions?

Models are trained on peer-reviewed public datasets and evaluated on held-out test sets. Accuracy varies by condition — see the Model Cards page for detailed metrics.

What medical reports can I upload?

PDF or image files (JPG, PNG) of blood test results, health checkup reports, or clinical summaries. Max size: 5 MB.

Do I need to create an account?

No. The tool is fully free and requires no login.

Can I use this for someone else?

Yes. You can enter data on behalf of a family member. Results are not linked to any identity.
