About

What is HealthPredict AI?

HealthPredict AI is a preventive screening interface that combines structured risk forms, medical report-assisted entry, trained machine learning models, and contextual explanations to help users understand population-level health risk signals.

Our Vision

Democratizing preventive healthcare

Preventive health technology should be accessible, explainable, and fast enough to help people ask better questions before risk becomes crisis.

Data Collection

Data sources and collection methodology

Model training data is sourced from publicly available, peer-reviewed or widely used clinical datasets. Each dataset contains anonymized, de-identified patient records suitable for population-level statistical modeling.

Important Medical Disclaimer

The predictions generated by HealthPredict AI are statistical estimates based on machine learning models trained on population-level clinical datasets. These results are NOT a medical diagnosis and should NOT be used as a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Always consult a qualified doctor or visit an accredited pathology laboratory for accurate medical testing and clinical evaluation. If you are experiencing any symptoms or health concerns, seek immediate professional medical attention.
